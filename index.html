a boxing shop where you describe what you need and it finds the glove.

14keyword rules

first-time buyers don't know what to filter by, so you just type what you do. the parser pulls out budget, bodyweight, glove weight and discipline, treats budget as a hard limit, then ranks what's left. whole-word matching matters: a plain substring check reads "twice" as the colour "ice" and "padding" as pad work. try it.

it started as a concept i made off my own back. the owner saw it, approved it, and is putting the design live on rawcagesports.com in the near future.
